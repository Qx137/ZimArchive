# **App Name**: ZimArchive

## Core Features:

- Presidential Timeline: Display a historical timeline of the President of Zimbabwe's life and career.
- Achievements Showcase: Showcase key achievements and initiatives of the President and his party, categorized by sector (e.g., economy, education, health).
- Historical Context: Provide historical context of the political party, including its origins, key figures, and historical milestones.
- Image Gallery: A gallery of photographs and videos documenting the President's activities and party events.
- AI-Powered Fact Checker: Utilize a tool to check and validate statements made by the President and about the party, providing sources and context.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep burgundy (#800020) to reflect the gravitas and history.
- Background color: Off-white (#F5F0E1) to create a clean and readable layout.
- Accent color: Gold (#FFD700) for highlights and important elements, symbolizing legacy and value.
- Body font: 'Literata', serif, for a literary and vintage feel suitable for displaying historical information.
- Headline font: 'Belleza', sans-serif, to add personality, especially suited to headers in fashion and art context. Using Literata for the body, allows contrast in font styles that doesn't impede readability.
- Use official and symbolic icons relevant to Zimbabwe and its political history.
- Subtle transitions and animations to enhance user engagement without distracting from the content.
